# README: A2A Prototype Synthetic Data

*Synthetic data for an illustrative prototype. No real accounts, people or events.*

## What this is

A month of invented settlement activity (trade dates 20 Oct to 18 Nov 2026, settlement through 19 Nov 2026) across four organizations, stored the way operations teams actually keep it: system extracts in spreadsheets, procedures in documents, and coordination in email and chat exports. It feeds the BNY × CMU A2A settlement prototype (see the PRD), but it is not limited to the demo case: about 2,300 BNY settlement instructions and 1,850 Goldman Sachs trades sit around it.

Real firm names give context. People, accounts, funds and every record are invented. Tickers, CUSIPs and ISINs of well-known US equities are real public identifiers (`id_source = public`). DTC participant numbers 0901 (BNY) and 0005 (Goldman Sachs) are real; all others are synthetic. Email addresses use `.test` domains. The data was generated by a seeded script (seed 20261119), so it can be regenerated identically.

## Golden case: the NVDA DK

AllianzGI Global Equity Fund (synthetic), fund AGI-GLEQ, BNY account 4471-AGI-GLEQ, buys 10,000 NVDA (US67066G1040) from Goldman Sachs at $186.25. Trade date 18 Nov 2026, settles 19 Nov. At DTC it is DVP from Goldman (0005) to BNY (0901); for BNY it is a receive (RVP). Net $1,862,600.00: $1,862,500 plus $100 commission ($0.01/share).

| 18 Nov 20:40 CET | AllianzGI | The PM tops the order up to 10,000 (the orders export shows the current 10,000) |

| 18 Nov 20:58 CET | AllianzGI | Allocation v1 (ALC-26-322-0773-1) goes out at the stale **8,000**, before the 21:00 CET custodian cutoff |

| 18 Nov 21:03 CET | CTM | Goldman's confirm CTM-77104562 (10,000) doesn't match the 8,000 allocation |
| 18 Nov 23:40 CET | AllianzGI | E. Carter amends the allocation to 10,000 (ALC-26-322-0773-2) and affirms CTM at 10,000 (22:52 UTC). The amended custodian instruction to BNY is queued for approval and **never released** (NOT_SENT) |
| 19 Nov 08:02 ET | DTC | Near-miss: Lakeshore's 8,000 NVDA from Goldman settles cleanly (DTC-DO-20261119-000301) |

| 19 Nov 08:21 ET onward | BNY / Goldman | Ops chat and Goldman email thread: neither side can quickly find the right contact at the other. AllianzGI must re-instruct by 11:30 ET for same-day settlement |

BNY cannot change a client instruction on its own: only AllianzGI can re-instruct. The correct counterparty SSI is SSI-GS-US-EQ-0005 (ACTIVE). The data is a snapshot at 09:00 ET on 19 Nov with the case unresolved: the prototype starts from there. In the live case AllianzGI's trade operations (Emily Carter) re-instructs, BNY's corrected instruction arrives and Goldman redelivers; those records are created by the prototype, not stored here.

**Near-misses built in, so an agent has to check rather than pattern-match:**

- A second Goldman NVDA trade the same day for a different BNY client (Lakeshore Teachers' Pension Plan, 8,000 shares at 186.31, $1,490,560, BNYI-20261118-5527388, DTC-DO-20261119-000301), which settled at 08:02 ET. Same quantity as BNY's golden instruction, $480 apart in amount.
- A superseded Goldman SSI with a different participant number (SSI-GS-US-EQ-7108, ended 2024-05-31).
- Another AllianzGI trade on 18 Nov, 5,000 AVGO for AGI-TECH with Morgan Stanley (AGI-ORD-26-322-0781), held in repair (RQ-20261118-0407, INVALID_SSI, pending verification of Morgan Stanley's new SSI; past AllianzGI's 60-minute repair SLA).

## Built-in patterns

- About 6% of instructions go to repair. The digital employee DE-REPAIR-01 handles only MISSING_SEC_ID, resolves roughly an eighth of all repair items (about half of the MISSING_SEC_ID ones), and rejects when reference data has no single match. People work the queue 07:30–17:30 ET; items that arrive later wait for the morning. AllianzGI items open more than 60 minutes note the SLA breach.
- About 4% of instructions fail. Each fail shows the same way at every firm: DTC records a DK (QTY_MISMATCH, SSI_MISMATCH) or a PENDING (INSUFFICIENT_POSITION, UNAFFIRMED, HOLD, NET_DEBIT_CAP); BNY and Goldman open matching fail records; when a fail is resolved, DTC shows it SETTLED (after a DK, a redelivery under the same reference first). Goldman's fail reason and next action depend on whether Goldman delivers or receives.
- Positions are built from settled history, so every client sale settling 19 Nov is covered unless it has an open lack-of-securities fail. pending_receipts and pending_deliveries list 19 Nov settlements.
- Equity settlement amounts include $0.01/share commission everywhere (buyers pay it, sellers net it). Bonds settle at price × face / 100; Goldman books its bond trades on the US Credit desk.
- About a third of AllianzGI equity orders are blocks across 2–3 funds (fund_code BLOCK), with one allocation, BNY instruction and Goldman delivery per fund. Buy limits sit above the fill and sell limits below. AllianzGI allocations reach BNY before its 21:00 CET same-day cutoff.
- Each non-BNY Goldman client settles through one custodian, the one in Goldman's SSI book. Affirmation is done in CTM by the client (or its custodian), not by Goldman; a late affirmation is what causes an UNAFFIRMED fail.
- Times are in each firm's real time zone, daylight saving included (US EDT until 1 Nov, Europe CEST until 25 Oct).
- 11 Nov (Veterans Day): equities trade and settle at DTC; Fedwire and the bond market are closed, so no bond trades or settlements that day.
- DISC drafts about a quarter of client inquiry replies, in about 25 seconds against about 90 for a person.
- US Treasuries settle on Fedwire, so they are not in the DTC feed.
- A few superseded and cancelled rows, blank optional fields and free-text ops comments are included on purpose.

## How to export for the prototype repo

1. Download each spreadsheet tab (not the README tab) as CSV to `/data/<firm>/<tab>.csv`, where `<firm>` is `bny`, `goldman`, `dtc` or `allianzgi` and `<tab>` is the tab name.
2. Download each Doc as Markdown to `/data/<firm>/docs/<snake_case_title>.md`.
3. Export DATA_DICTIONARY to `/data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`.

A ready-made copy of the whole `/data` folder was delivered alongside this estate, so this step is only needed after editing the Google files.
