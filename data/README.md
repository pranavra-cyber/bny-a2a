# README: A2A Prototype Synthetic Data

*Synthetic data for an illustrative prototype. No real accounts, people or events.*

## What this is

A month of invented settlement activity (trade dates 20 Oct to 18 Nov 2026, settlement through 19 Nov 2026) across four organizations, stored the way operations teams actually keep it: system extracts in spreadsheets, procedures in documents, and coordination in email and chat exports. It feeds the BNY × CMU A2A settlement prototype (see the PRD), but it is not limited to the demo case: about 2,200 BNY settlement instructions and 1,900 Goldman Sachs trades sit around it.

Real firm names give context. People, accounts, funds and every record are invented. Tickers, CUSIPs and ISINs of well-known US equities are real public identifiers (`id_source = public`). DTC participant numbers 0901 (BNY) and 0005 (Goldman Sachs) are real; all others are synthetic. Email addresses use `.test` domains. The data was generated by a seeded script (seed 20261119), so it can be regenerated identically.

## Golden case: the NVDA DK

AllianzGI Global Equity Fund (synthetic), fund AGI-GLEQ, BNY account 4471-AGI-GLEQ, buys 10,000 NVDA (US67066G1040) from Goldman Sachs at $186.25. Trade date 18 Nov 2026, settles 19 Nov, DVP. Net $1,862,600.00.

| 18 Nov 21:10 CET | AllianzGI | Allocation v1 for 8,000 sent to BNY (ALC-26-322-0773-1, order AGI-ORD-26-322-0773) |
| 18 Nov 15:10 ET | BNY | Instruction BNYI-20261118-5527301 for 8,000, counterparty participant 0005, SENT_TO_DTC |
| 18 Nov 23:38 CET | AllianzGI → BNY | Email: amendment to 10,000 |

The correct counterparty SSI is SSI-GS-US-EQ-0005 (ACTIVE). The data ends around 09:00 ET on 19 Nov with nothing resolved: the prototype starts from there.

**Near-misses built in, so an agent has to check rather than pattern-match:**

- A second Goldman NVDA trade the same day for a different BNY client (Lakeshore Teachers' Pension Plan, 8,000 shares, BNYI-20261118-5527388, DTC-DO-20261119-000301), which settles cleanly.
- A superseded Goldman SSI with a different participant number (SSI-GS-US-EQ-7108, ended 2024-05-31).
- Another AllianzGI trade on 18 Nov with Morgan Stanley that is also in repair (RQ-20261118-0407, INVALID_SSI, pending SSI verification).

## Built-in patterns

- About 6% of instructions go to repair. The digital employee DE-REPAIR-01 handles only MISSING_SEC_ID, resolves roughly a fifth of all repair items, and rejects when reference data has no single match.
- About 4% of instructions fail; lack of securities is the most common reason.
- DISC drafts about a quarter of client inquiry replies, in about 25 seconds against about 90 for a person.
- US Treasuries settle on Fedwire, so they are not in the DTC feed.
- A few superseded and cancelled rows, blank optional fields and free-text ops comments are included on purpose.

## How to export for the prototype repo

1. Download each spreadsheet tab (not the README tab) as CSV to `/data/<firm>/<tab>.csv`, where `<firm>` is `bny`, `goldman`, `dtc` or `allianzgi` and `<tab>` is the tab name.
2. Download each Doc as Markdown to `/data/<firm>/docs/<snake_case_title>.md`.
3. Export DATA_DICTIONARY to `/data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`.

A ready-made copy of the whole `/data` folder was delivered alongside this estate, so this step is only needed after editing the Google files.
